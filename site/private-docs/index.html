the short version

The private version of pasting it into ChatGPT.

Upload a raw document and the whole thing — SSN and all — lands on a company's servers, where it may be kept or used for training. AI DocPrep keeps the sensitive parts on your machine and hands the chatbot only the text you're comfortable sharing.

what it catches

The details you'd never want on a server.

Out of the box, with no setup, it removes:

  • The obvious. SSNs, account and card numbers, emails, phone numbers, addresses.
  • Names, with on-device AI. A bundled model catches people, places, and organizations.
  • Anything you add. List your own terms — a case number, a nickname — to always scrub.
